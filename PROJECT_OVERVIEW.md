# 🏙️ CivicFix AI — Smart Civic & Campus Incident Triage Platform

> An intelligent, human-centered public issue resolution system that reads incoming citizen complaints, assesses urgency, eliminates duplicate reports, and routes tasks to the right municipal and campus departments.

---

## 📌 1. What is CivicFix AI? (Its Core Use)

CivicFix AI is an automated triage and operations management platform built for municipal corporations, public service agencies, and university campuses. 

Every day, cities and large campuses receive hundreds of complaints about broken roads, leaking water pipes, exposed electrical wiring, overflowing garbage, and hazardous open manholes. Managing these manually leads to delayed emergency responses, wasted contractor budgets, and citizen frustration.

**CivicFix AI acts as an intelligent first-responder and digital dispatch coordinator**:
- It reads citizen-submitted reports and images.
- It identifies the exact nature of the problem and how dangerous it is.
- It checks if someone else has already reported the exact same incident so teams don't waste time and money visiting the same location twice.
- It recommends the appropriate maintenance unit, estimated repair timeframe, and recommended action.
- It presents these insights to a human administrator who retains complete authority to approve, adjust, or reject the recommendation before any contractor is hired.

## 👥 2. Who is it Used By? (Target Users)

CivicFix AI brings together three key stakeholder groups into one unified ecosystem:

### 1. Citizens & Campus Residents
- Everyday citizens, students, and campus staff who encounter neighborhood or campus hazards.
- They get an accessible, multi-lingual tool to report problems in seconds with real-time status tracking so they know their complaints aren't lost in bureaucracy.

### 2. Public Agencies & Campus Administrators (Human Reviewers)
- Municipal officers, campus facility managers, and dispatch coordinators.
- They get an intelligent Command Center that filters noise, groups duplicates, highlights life-threatening emergencies, and automates department assignments while keeping final approvals in their hands.

### 3. Field Contractors & Maintenance Units
- Electricians, plumbers, road repair contractors, and sanitation teams.
- They receive clear, pre-triaged job orders with assigned budgets, locations, and priority levels, eliminating confusion in the field.

## 🌟 3. Key Features

### 🚨 Smart Urgency Scoring & Safety Advisory
- Instantly categorizes complaints into **Critical**, **High**, **Medium**, or **Low** urgency with a transparent 1–100 severity score.
- Automatically provides temporary public safety precautions (e.g., safety distance from a fallen live wire or warnings around open sewer pits) so citizens know how to stay safe while repair crews are en route.

### 👁️ True Visual Duplicate Detection (Zero False Positives)
- Checks whether two incoming reports share the exact same photo or refer to the exact same incident at the same address.
- **Intelligent Differentiation**: It carefully distinguishes between two *different* potholes on two different streets. It will never falsely merge separate valid issues just because they happen to be in the same problem category.
- Duplicate reports are neatly clustered under the original report so administrators can review and resolve them together with a single click.

### 🏢 Automatic Department Routing & Cost Estimation
- Identifies whether a job requires an **Electrician**, **Plumber**, **Road Maintenance Crew**, **Garbage Collector**, or **General Maintenance Unit**.
- Suggests estimated resolution turnaround times and baseline pricing to streamline contractor hiring.

### 🛠️ Public DIY & Minimum Labour Filtering
- Not every minor issue requires spending contracted taxpayer or campus funds.
- Administrators can review minor tasks that fall below the contractor labor threshold (such as clearing dry leaves off a surface drain grate) and reject them with clear, safe self-help guidance for the community.
- These rejected items remain private to the administration and do not clutter public tracking feeds.

### 🌐 Citizen-First Experience & Multi-Language Support
- Full accessibility in **English**, **Hindi**, and **Marathi** so non-English speakers can easily file reports.
- Includes a smooth, hands-free auto-scrolling Community Reports feed that citizens can browse to see recent neighborhood fixes without manual scrolling.

## 🧭 5. How It Works (Step-by-Step Experience)

### Step 1: Reporting an Issue (Citizen)
1. A citizen spots an issue (e.g., an open manhole or water leak) and opens the portal.
2. They select the issue category, take a picture, enter a short description, and select the location.
3. The platform immediately provides practical safety advice (e.g., *"Place a warning stick nearby; keep children away"*).

### Step 2: Intelligent Triage (AI First-Responder)
1. The AI reads the report, analyzes the image, and determines the danger level.
2. It cross-checks existing neighborhood reports:
   - If an identical photo or the exact same spot has already been submitted, it flags it as a 100% duplicate.
   - If it is a new, unique problem, it assigns priority and recommends the right department.

### Step 3: Review & Dispatch (Human Officer)
1. The administrator reviews the incoming complaint in the Command Center.
2. The administrator can:
   - **Accept & Dispatch**: Assigns the job to the recommended department with an allocated budget.
   - **Merge Duplicate**: Merges repetitive reports into the original incident.
   - **Reject (Public DIY)**: Marks minor tasks for community self-resolution if they don't justify contractor callout costs.

### Step 4: Field Resolution (Contractor)
1. The assigned maintenance unit accepts the work order.
2. The crew arrives on site, fixes the problem, and updates the status to "Resolved".

### Step 5: Live Notification (Public Feed)
1. The citizen tracks their report status through to completion.
2. The resolved fix appears on the community feed, showcasing active neighborhood maintenance and accountable civic governance.
